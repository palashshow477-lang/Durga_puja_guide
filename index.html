Kolkata Durga Puja Pandals

A Guide to Famous Heritage & Contemporary Art Pandals

Durga Puja in Kolkata is a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage festival. Every year, local communities transform the city into an open-air art gallery with magnificently themed pandals, dazzling lighting work, and traditional rituals.

Iconic North Kolkata Pandals (Traditional & Cultural)

1. Ahiritola Sarbojanin Durgotsab
📍 Location: Ahiritola, North Kolkata | 🚆 Nearest Metro: Shobhabazar Sutanuti

Heritage Renowned for deeply artistic themes depicting social awareness, Indian mythology, and traditional street art. Operating since 1940.

2. Sovabazar Rajbari
📍 Location: Raja Nabakrishna Street, Sovabazar | 🚆 Nearest Metro: Shobhabazar Sutanuti

Aristocratic Tradition Started by Raja Nabakrishna Deb in 1757, this is one of the oldest Bonedi Bari (aristocratic house) pujas in Kolkata, preserving authentic zamindari traditions.

3. Kumartuli Park
📍 Location: Kumartuli, North Kolkata | 🚆 Nearest Metro: Shobhabazar Sutanuti

Artisan Hub Located right in the heart of the potters' quarter where idols are sculpted. Known for creative fusing of traditional idol craftsmanship with modern grandeur.

Magnificent South Kolkata Pandals (Grand & Contemporary)

4. Chetla Agrani Club
📍 Location: Chetla, South Kolkata | 🚆 Nearest Metro: Kalighat

Award-Winning Art Celebrated for extraordinary conceptual architecture, immersive lighting, and world-class theme installations created by top Bengali artists.

5. Suruchi Sangha
📍 Location: New Alipore, South Kolkata | 🚆 Nearest Metro: Majerhat / Kalighat

State Themes Famous for showcasing the art, culture, and handicrafts of different Indian states through meticulous artistic pandal structures.

6. Ekdalia Evergreen Club
📍 Location: Gariahat, South Kolkata | 🚆 Nearest Metro: Ballygunge / Kalighat

Classic Splendor Famous for replicating ancient Indian temples with intricate interior chandeliers and traditional idol styling.

East Kolkata & Sreebhumi Mega Attractions

7. Sreebhumi Sporting Club
📍 Location: Lake Town, VIP Road | 🚌 Access: VIP Road / Bidhannagar

Opulent Architecture Famous for massive scale replicas of global monuments (e.g., Burj Khalifa, Vatican City, Disneyland) decked with real gold jewelry on the idols.

Useful Tips for Pandal Hopping

  • Metro Connectivity: Use the Kolkata Metro Blue Line for quick transit between North and South Kolkata hubs.
  • Peak Hours: Crowds peak between 7:00 PM and midnight. Early morning (5:00 AM – 9:00 AM) is best for peaceful viewing and photography.
  • Food & Street Eats: Enjoy local delicacies like Kathi Rolls, Phuchka, Mishti Doi, and Puja special Bhog prasad across stall setups.
